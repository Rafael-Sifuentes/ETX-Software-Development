Creamy Tomato & Spinach Meatballs

Comfort food at its finest. Seared beef meatballs simmered in a rich, orange-pink creamy tomato sauce packed with fresh spinach and parmesan.

What You'll Need

The Meatballs

  • Ground Beef: 1 lb (80/20 preferred)
  • Binder: 1 large egg + ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • Seasoning: Salt, pepper, garlic powder, parsley

The Sauce

  • Fats: Olive oil & 2 Tbsp Butter
  • Aromatics: 1 onion (diced), 3-4 cloves garlic
  • Base: 2 Tbsp Tomato paste, pinch red pepper flakes
  • Liquid: 1½ cups Heavy Cream + Pasta water
  • Finish: Spinach, Parmesan, Flour (2 Tbsp)

Step-by-Step Instructions

Phase 1: Prep & Sear

1
Mix the Dough In a large glass bowl, combine beef, egg, breadcrumbs, and dry seasonings. Mix gently by hand until just combined.
2
Roll & Sear Roll into golf-ball sizes. Heat oil in a deep skillet. Sear meatballs on all sides until browned (don't cook fully through). Remove and set aside.

Phase 2: The Sauce Base

3
Sauté Aromatics In the same pan (keep the fat!), melt butter. Add onions and garlic. Sauté until soft.
4
Cook the Paste Add tomato paste and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1-2 minutes to remove the raw metallic flavor.

Phase 3: Cream & Simmer

5
Make the Roux Sprinkle flour over the onion mixture. Stir to coat. Slowly pour in heavy cream while whisking to create a smooth orange-pink sauce.
6
Season & Thin Add herbs. Ladle in starchy boiling water from your pasta pot to reach desired consistency.
7
Finish It Off Add meatballs back in. Add spinach and stir until wilted. Top with parmesan and simmer 5-10 mins until meatballs are cooked through.

Don't plate dry noodles! Toss your cooked drained pasta in a large bowl with two ladles of the finished sauce before plating. This ensures every bite is flavorful.
